Drop the WAD files. An IWAD is a whole game, a PWAD adds levels to one, and its maps say which
game: Hexen's have scripts, the rest play on Doom, then Heretic.

#### Doom

```text
doom.wad                  Doom, or The Ultimate Doom
doom2.wad                 Doom II
tnt.wad                   Final Doom: TNT
plutonia.wad              Final Doom: Plutonia
doom1.wad                 the shareware episode
mymaps.wad                a PWAD, on the IWAD it fits
```

#### Heretic

```text
heretic.wad               Heretic, or Shadow of the Serpent Riders
heretic1.wad              the shareware episode
```

#### Hexen

```text
hexen.wad                 Hexen, or its four-level demo
hexdd.wad                 Deathkings, played on hexen.wad
```
